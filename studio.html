The studio

History Twisted into Fantasy

Inside Ithaca Games, we believe the most profound tales emerge from history's depths.

Our studio masters narrative-rich tabletop gaming, where fallen empires and mythic sagas collide through tactical mechanics and immersive world-building.

We transform historical complexity into polished, grand journeys that resonate with dedicated tacticians and new adventurers looking for a truly legendary experience.

We twist history into fantasy and chaos into cardboard. As you set out for Ithaca, the voyage should be a long one, so we build games that make the journey worth it!
