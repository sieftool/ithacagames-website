Our debut game

Reclaim the Land. Reshape the Legend.

The Han Empire has fallen. Dong Zhuo rules from the shadows of the throne. The Yellow Turban Rebellion spreads like wildfire across the provinces. And as the realm descends into chaos, the great forces of Wei, Wu, and Shu-Han rise to claim their place in the storm to come.

But not all stories are written in the scrolls of history. In ASSERTION: The Three Kingdoms players step into the turmoil of a fractured China, drawing from the legendary Romance of the Three Kingdoms to explore a bold, re-imagined path through one of history's most turbulent eras. New warlords rise. Loyalties shift. The fate of the land may yet be shaped by unexpected hands.

Players
1–4
Playing time
90 min
Age
14+

An action-selection, area-majority, conflict-euro hybrid. Game design by George Englezos, George Gkartzonikas, Hent van Imhoff and Michalis Nikolaou. Art by Pauline Derouinau and Joel Holtzman.

The studio

History Twisted into Fantasy

Inside Ithaca Games, we believe the most profound tales emerge from history's depths.

Our studio masters narrative-rich tabletop gaming, where fallen empires and mythic sagas collide through tactical mechanics and immersive world-building.

We transform historical complexity into polished, grand journeys that resonate with dedicated tacticians and new adventurers looking for a truly legendary experience.

We twist history into fantasy and chaos into cardboard. As you set out for Ithaca, the voyage should be a long one, so we build games that make the journey worth it!
