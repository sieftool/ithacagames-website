About the game

The Han Empire has fallen. Dong Zhuo rules from the shadows of the throne. The Yellow Turban Rebellion spreads like wildfire across the provinces. And as the realm descends into chaos, the great forces of Wei, Wu, and Shu-Han rise to claim their place in the storm to come.

But not all stories are written in the scrolls of history. In ASSERTION: The Three Kingdoms players step into the turmoil of a fractured China, drawing from the legendary Romance of the Three Kingdoms to explore a bold, re-imagined path through one of history's most turbulent eras. New warlords rise. Loyalties shift. The fate of the land may yet be shaped by unexpected hands.

Game design by George Englezos, George Gkartzonikas, Hent van Imhoff and Michalis Nikolaou. Art by Pauline Derouinau and Joel Holtzman.

The path to power is yours to carve.

Command armies, court favour, reshape alliances and navigate the ambitions of kingdoms and emperors alike.

  • In battle

    Recruit units for your legion, invade the regions of China and win battles to raise your Watchtowers.

  • In diplomacy

    Make treaties with the Kingdoms to earn their trust and create alliances.

  • And in shadows

    Assign your Eunuchs to the Palace and manoeuvre around the intrigues of the court.

Three Kingdoms stake claim. So do you.
